# Roadmap

Xylarium is pre-alpha. This roadmap opens with the path from where the
universe stands to where it is going, then the mathematics the laws are
built on, then the milestones in intended order. Each milestone states
its goal, why it comes where it does, what has landed, what is next, and
the `xylarium observe` reading that shows it done. Changes are recorded in
[CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md). To build, found and run a universe, see
the [README](../README.md#quick-start).

## The path

**Where it is going.** A universe whose physics can be changed trivially to
test a theory (one law or one ruler swapped in its package, founded, and
compared reading by reading), and in which intelligence is built across
the cosmos in realms distributed on equal laws, as solar systems and
collections of constellations in every galaxy.

**Where it stands (2026-11-10).** The founding is sound in the founding calibre:
the bounce, the elements and atoms, the firmament, the paired core and its
spin (T16, T17, T19), the ocean's held medium. Above it the physics does not
hold:
- Gravity is read as a share on a fixed manifold, one face per site, not
  as the deformation about a mass invariant with both faces
  ([#155](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/xylarium/issues/155)).
- Readings take one face of the dipole `3Z/3Q + R`, `3Q/3Z - R` and drop the
  integer relations a turn carries ([#148](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/xylarium/issues/148), [#149](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/xylarium/issues/149)).
- Two calibres are mixed in one deficit ([#123](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/xylarium/issues/123)), so stars read deficits
  of `2.4e-11` and close worlds 240 to 400 pc out ([#129](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/xylarium/issues/129)).
- Turns are counted in rings and radians ([#127](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/xylarium/issues/127)).
- Bodies are bands, circles and points, not the vortices the founding
  derives ([#121](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/xylarium/issues/121)).
- The five planes are held as one number ([#128](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/xylarium/issues/128)).
- Earth and Milky Way rulers remain at the SI seam ([#25](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/xylarium/issues/25)).
- One process holds one universe ([#142](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/xylarium/issues/142)), and no reading compares two.

## Mathematics

Xylarium holds no floating-point state: `clippy::float_arithmetic` is denied
across the workspace. The number systems, the algebra and the nucleon model
below carry every law; [EQUATIONS.md](EQUATIONS.md) gives each formula with
its source location.

### Number systems

| System | Role | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Integers (ZZ) | Everything counted: protons and neutrons, whole nuclei, states, S^11 harmonics, the seal counts, the sexagesimal places of a coordinate, seeds and stream states | `u32` / `i64` indices; counts beyond a machine word as `Q` with denominator 1 (`num-bigint`); `atoms`, `fibres`, `circuit::grain`, `xyl_core::coordinate`, `xyl_core::rng` |
| Rationals (QQ) | All held state: element counts, firmament and bead amounts, reaches, spans, phases, the anchor, star and world masses | `xyl_core::Q`, lowest terms, `i64` on the stack and promoted to big integers on overflow; division is checked |
| Reals (RR) | Never held directly. A real value enters as a proven rational enclosure `lo <= x <= hi` (pi, roots, exp, ln, sin, cos), or as a dyadic reading `m 2^e` at a seam, rounded toward zero and gridded back to `Q` before it is held | `xyl_core::certified::Encl`; `xyl_core::dyadic::Dy` |

Angles are exact degrees on a named plane, read in sectors of base 60 and written in degrees
and their places (`xyl_core::coordinate`, LAW-COORDINATE v1). Phases about a throat are exact; drawn values
carry the `2^-40` resolution of the draw.

### Algebra and geometry

- Geometric algebra `Cl(p, q)` over `Q` (`xyl_core::cga`), the signature
  carried by the multivector's type; its conformal model `Cl(n+1, 1)`
  provides points, versors and dilators. The nucleon poles described below
  are its origin and its point at infinity.
- Rotors (`xyl_core::rotor::Rotor`) are even versors of `xyl_core::cga`
  over `N` base axes, the dimension held in the type. A turn names its
  plane by its two generators and is stated in degrees
  (`rotor::Turn`); a drawn half-angle direction is refused once, where it
  is drawn, if zero (`rotor::Direction`), and a plane written in the code
  is checked when the code is compiled (`Rotor::on`). Every manifold holds
  its plane as an exact rotor, and a rotor turns a vector by the sandwich
  `V v V~ / (V V~)` (`Rotor::apply`), never by a matrix.
- Sphere measure (`xyl_core::measure`) gives the share of an n-sphere
  within an angle of an axis, on which caps and shares on a manifold are
  based.
- Harmonics on S^11 (`fibres`, `atoms`): degree `l` has
  `d(l) = C(l + 11, 11) - C(l + 9, 11)` directions (1, 12, 77, 352, ...).
  Shells and seals follow by exact binomial sums.
- Motion (`xyl_core::motion`, `xyl_core::event`): every change is a
  one-parameter motion with an exact phase at `t`. Boosts are modes
  `amp x base^(rate t)` with exact rapidity, and crossings are solved with
  certified bounds.
- Units (`xyl_core::units`) are `G = c = M_core = 1`, with the length unit
  `r_g` and one power of sixty per tier of the anchor chain.

### Nucleons: 3Z, 3Q and the polarity R

A kind is a nuclide `(Z, Q)`, where `Z` counts its protons and `Q` its
neutrons. Its mass is `Z + Q` nucleons, which gravity and inertia read
(`circuit::nucleus_weights`, `formation::mass_weight`). Chemistry, the
founding and the light read the capacity's occupancy instead (axiom 9).

- **3Z, the span.** The built account gives each proton three units, split
  between the Z pole (held, coherence) and the Q pole (open, decoherence):
  the held count `w = 3Z - u`, `u` the open (unpaired) states
  (`compound::electron_hold`, `compound::open`). Pairing moves an open state to the
  held side with the span fixed. The closed share `h_Z = (3Z - u_Z) / 3Z`
  drives the capture chain (`elements::closed_share`). Amounts move in whole
  nuclei of `w`, so fusion conserves 3Z (`circuit::grain::nuclei_of`), and
  the stars' transduction produces light with 3Z conserved. Three per
  proton is a delimiter, not a capacity: axiom 9 restates the account on
  the capacity, `cap = E + U + 2P`, where the 3 cancels from every share
  (`3Z / 3Q = Z / cap`) ([#158](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/xylarium/issues/158)).
- **3Q, the capacity.** 3Q is three times the number of states in every
  shell the atom reaches: `cap = sum_n sum_(l < n) 2 d(l)` and
  `3Q = 3 cap`. The balance `R_Q = 6Z - 3Q` and its share
  `rho_Q = (2Z - cap) / cap` mark the seals where `rho_Q = +1`, at Z 2, 28,
  208 and 1092 (`xylarium observe seals`).
- **R, the polarity.** The electron phase state
  `R = kappa (1 - 2 frac(p))` lies in `[-kappa, kappa]`. Its sign is the
  hemisphere of the bounce's pulse `p` (a share of 360 degrees on the
  isoclinic planes), and its magnitude is the
  electrons' coherence on S^11, `kappa = 2 G / ends` (`fibres`). It is zero
  for a kind with no charge (`circuit::electron_phase`).
- **The pole point.** A carrier's pole state is the conformal point `X(t)`
  at `t = u / w`, open over held. The Z pole is the origin `e_o`, the Q
  pole is the point at infinity `e_inf`, and the throat is at `t = 1`. The
  vacuum's inversion `X(t) -> X(1 / t)` swaps the poles. An echo's flux is
  the dilator `D(rho)`, toward Z where `rho < 1` and toward Q where
  `rho > 1`, and the books close where the composed channel versors equal
  the observed one (`circuit/poles.rs`).

## 1. Founding in the core's units (done)

Goal: A universe is founded from its package and its seed in the universal

founding.

first.

  founding and are not part of it.

- Galactic cores are held in the universal core's units. A galactic core's
  mass is `hole_mass x state` (`cores::founding_of`), and
  `Substrate::holds_a_star` reads the bead's state directly; neither
  divides by the mean bead state.

Done when: `xylarium observe galactic-core N` reads each galactic core's

## 3. Rules from the founding and the galactic cores (current)

display, through `package::Si`, and the universal physical constants (c,
G, k_B, h) are the only measured values the laws read.

Why this order: The layers above the founding were ported from a prototype that

the galactic cores in the same change. A law with no derivation yet is not
carried over in a new unit; it is listed under the layer as open.

  appears only in `package::Si` for display and for the universal
  constants (c, G, k_B, h).

  shares summing to one, ordering), not by agreement with earlier readings.

  the ley lines per reading (`LINES_PER_READING`, 1024) are resolution
  choices.
- **Grids** (the resolution a quantity is cut to toward zero, `2^-b`, by
  `xyl_core::certified::toward_zero`): 64 for
  a nucleus's neutrons read for its weight (`circuit::rest_neutrons`), the
  founding shells' reaches, swings and rest points (`formation::founding_shells`),
  the turning taken (`formation::spin_orbit`), the
  curved reach (`bounce::curved_reach`) and star masses
  (`galaxy/star.rs` MASS_BITS); 60 for field readings (`field.rs` GRID),
  star radii (`galaxy/star.rs`) and the turning per ring
  (`turning::turning_si`); 48 for element abundances (`elements.rs`
  GRID_BITS); 40 for the origin, the drawn spin, galaxy and centre
  readings (`origin.rs`, `galaxy/mod.rs`, `centre.rs` GRID_BITS); 32 for
  bodies, isotopes and orbits (`bodies/mod.rs` GRID); and the nucleus
  grain's 8 bits below one hydrogen of the founding count
  (`circuit/grain.rs` nucleon_unit). Each is to be derived from the state
  count of its carrier (item 66, [#210](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/xylarium/issues/210)).
